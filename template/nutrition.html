HEALTHY NUTRITION

🥗 Nutrition Guide

Simple and balanced food choices to support your daily activity.

🍳

Breakfast

Idli with sambar, oats with fruit, or eggs with whole-grain toast.

🍎

Healthy Snack

Choose fruits, curd, nuts or other simple nutritious snacks.

🍛

Lunch

Rice or roti with vegetables, dal, curd and a protein-rich food.

🥗

Dinner

Include vegetables, a protein-rich food and your usual staple food.

💧

Hydration

Drink water regularly throughout the day, especially around physical activity.

🌈

Eat Variety

Try to include different vegetables, fruits, grains and protein-rich foods.
